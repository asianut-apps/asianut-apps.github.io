1. What the app does

Pocket QR is a free tool for scanning and creating codes on your phone. It lets you:

  • scan QR codes and common barcodes with your camera, one at a time or several in a row (batch);
  • scan a QR code or barcode from an image you choose;
  • read printed text from a photo, on your device;
  • create QR codes for text, websites, Wi-Fi networks and contact cards, style them, and save or share them as images;
  • join a Wi-Fi network from a scanned Wi-Fi code, open a scanned website, search the web for a scanned result, or copy and share it;
  • keep an optional history of scans on your device (off by default);
  • add a “Scan QR” tile to Android’s Quick Settings.

Scanning, reading text and creating codes work without an internet connection. Opening websites, web searches and loading website icons in History need one. The app has no accounts and does not send your codes to our servers.

2. Permissions

The app uses your camera to scan codes and capture text, and only while a scanning screen is open. It uses internet access to open links and to load website icons in History. It uses Wi-Fi access only to hand a scanned Wi-Fi network to Android when you choose to connect. Images are opened only when you pick them in Android’s photo picker. You can change permissions at any time in your phone’s settings; some features will not work without them.
