Pocket QR Privacy Policy

Developer: ASIANUT · Effective date: 6 November 2026 · Contact: okellodeveloper@gmail.com

Pocket QR scans and creates QR codes and reads barcodes and text on your device. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your codes stay on your phone, and we do not run servers that receive them.

Camera

The camera is used only while a scanning screen is open, to read QR codes and barcodes, or to take a photo for reading text. Camera images are processed on your device and are not saved or uploaded by Pocket QR.

Photos

When you scan from a photo, Android's photo picker opens and Pocket QR receives only the single image you choose. The app does not ask for access to your whole photo library. The chosen image is read on your device to find a code and is not stored or uploaded.

Reading text

In Text mode, the photo you capture is read on your device to pull out its text. The photo is not saved or uploaded by Pocket QR. You decide whether to copy, share or turn the text into a QR code.

History

History is off by default. If you turn it on, the contents of codes you scan are saved in the app's private storage on your device, up to 200 items. A code you create is added to history only if you tap to add it. Wi-Fi codes are never saved to history. You can delete single items or all history at any time. Uninstalling the app removes its history.

Website icons

When you view History while online, the app may contact the websites of saved links to fetch their small icons. Those websites can see the request and your IP address, as with any normal visit. Nothing is sent to a Pocket QR server.

Wi-Fi codes

When you choose to connect to a network from a scanned Wi-Fi code, the network name and password are handed to Android so it can join the network. Pocket QR does not send them anywhere else.

Sharing and saving

When you share or save a QR code or a result, it goes to the app or location you choose. Anyone who can see a Wi-Fi QR code can read its network name and password, so share those only with people you trust.

Opening links and web searches

Opening a scanned website, or using "Search the web" on a result, launches your browser or another app, and the scanned text is sent to that website or search engine. Their own privacy policies apply. Pocket QR cannot guarantee that a scanned link is safe, so check the address before opening it.

Google services in the app

Code reading and text reading use Google's ML Kit, which runs on your device. Google may collect limited diagnostic and usage information from ML Kit to keep it working well. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure for details.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics or tracking added by us.
  • We do not sell or share your information.

Children

Pocket QR is a general tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If the app's behaviour changes, for example if ads or purchases are added, we will update this policy and the date above.
