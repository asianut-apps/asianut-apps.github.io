Privacy Policy
  • Developer: ASIANUT
  • Effective: 18 November 2026
  • Contact: okellodeveloper@gmail.com

In short

  • Solo play needs no account. Your levels, stars, coins and settings stay on your phone.

  • Playing with friends is optional and uses Google sign-in. Only your first name and your score are shown to others in your room.

  • Ads are short videos you choose to watch for a reward. They come from Google AdMob.

  • We don't sell your information, and coins are never real money.

1Who we are

Flip Streak is a pairs memory game for Android made by ASIANUT (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Flip Streak app and to this page.

2Playing on your own

You don't need an account to play the levels. The game saves the following only on your phone:

  • your level records, stars, best scores, times and day streak;
  • your coins, and any Peeks and Hints you have collected;
  • the player name and avatar you choose in the game;
  • your settings, such as sound, vibration, dark mode and reduce motion;
  • a game you left part-way, so you can carry on later;
  • a skill estimate the game works out from how you play, used only to make the next levels easier or harder. It is calculated on your phone and never sent to us.

We cannot see any of this. It is removed if you use Reset progress in Settings, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the game.

3Playing with friends

Rooms for 2 to 6 players are optional. To use them you sign in with your Google account. We use Firebase, a Google service, to run rooms.

What we receive

  • From Google sign-in (Firebase Authentication): your Google account name, email address, profile picture and a user ID. These create your Flip Streak player account.
  • In a room (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions): the display name you play under (your first name by default, and you can change it), your user ID, the room code, your moves and turns, and scores and results.

Who can see it

Other players in your room see your display name, whose turn it is, the cards that are flipped and the scores. They do not see your email address. Rooms can only be read by the players in them.

Where it is kept

Room data is processed and stored by Google Cloud in the europe-west1 region (Belgium). Google's handling is covered by the Firebase privacy information. When the last player leaves a room, the room and its data are deleted.

Why

We use this information only to run multiplayer games, keep turns fair and show results.

4Ads

Flip Streak shows rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, and only when you choose to watch one: to refill an empty Peek or Hint, to double the coins from a level, or to collect the free daily pack. There are no banner ads, and no ads play on their own while you play.

When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. Google uses this to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, if you allow it, personalise them. Learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the wording varies by phone).
  • You never have to watch an ad. Every reward can be skipped with “No thanks”.

If we add other ad networks through AdMob, we will name them on this page first.

5Coins, Peeks and Hints

Coins are earned by winning stars and watching optional ads, and are spent in the in-game store on Peeks and Hints. They are virtual items with no money value. The game does not sell anything for real money, and we do not collect payment details.

6Permissions

  • Internet access: for rooms with friends, Google sign-in and ads. Solo play works offline.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.

The game does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

7What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • We don't read your Google contacts, Gmail or Drive.
  • We don't show your email address to other players.

8Keeping and deleting your information

  • On your phone: kept until you reset progress, clear the app's storage or uninstall.
  • Rooms: deleted when the last player leaves.
  • Your player account: kept while you use rooms. You can sign out at any time from the Friends screen.

Delete your account

Email okellodeveloper@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my Flip Streak account”. We will delete your player account and any room data linked to it within 30 days and confirm by email. Information Google AdMob holds is managed by Google and your Google account settings.

9Security

Information sent between the game and Firebase is encrypted in transit. Room data can only be changed through our server code, which checks every move, and the hidden board is never readable by anyone's phone. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

10Children

Flip Streak is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has signed in to play with friends, contact us and we will delete their account.

11Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to withdraw consent. To use these rights, email us. If you are in the EEA or the UK, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

For players in the EEA and the UK, we rely on: providing the service you ask for (to run rooms), your consent (for personalised ads), and our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and secure.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes or the law requires it. We will post the new version on this page and change the effective date. If a change is important, we will also tell you in the game.
