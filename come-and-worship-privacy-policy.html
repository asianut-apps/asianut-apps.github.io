Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · Applies to the Come and Worship app for Android (com.persmon.comeandworship)
In short
  • You don’t need an account, and we don’t ask for your name, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your bookmarks, notes, reading progress, searches and settings stay on your phone.
  • The app shows a small banner ad from Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device data, described below.
  • If you buy “Remove ads”, Google Play handles the payment. We never see your card or payment details.

1. Who we are

Come and Worship is an app of the Church of Uganda service book Come and Worship, made by the Come and Worship team (“we”, “us”). If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at ashabajasper@gmail.com.

2. Information kept only on your device

To make the app work, it saves the following on your phone. This information is not sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Your bookmarks and any notes you add to them
  • Your reading progress and the section you read last
  • Your recent searches
  • Your settings: text size, font, light or dark theme, vibration and sounds, and keeping the screen on
  • Whether you have bought “Remove ads”, and whether you have already seen the welcome screens

You can delete all of it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. Information collected by third-party services

The app uses the following Google services. They may collect information automatically when you use the app, under their own privacy policies.

Google AdMob (advertising)

The app shows a banner ad provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. AdMob may also collect diagnostic information to keep its service working. Depending on your settings and region, this may be used to show personalised ads.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads on some phones). Learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Google Play Billing (“Remove ads”)

If you buy the one-time “Remove ads” purchase, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy. The app only receives confirmation that the purchase was made, so it can stop showing ads. We do not receive your name, card or payment details.

4. Sharing and reporting mistakes

When you share or copy a section or a paragraph, the text goes only to the app you choose, such as WhatsApp or email.

When you use “Report a mistake”, the app opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. Nothing is sent until you press send. We receive only what you include, usually your name, email address and the description you write. We use it only to reply to you and to correct the app, and we don’t share it with anyone else. To have your messages deleted, email us at ashabajasper@gmail.com.

5. Permissions

The app uses these Android permissions:

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and to check purchases with Google Play. The book itself works fully offline.
  • Keep awake (wake lock): to keep the screen on while you read, if you turn that on.
  • Google Play billing: for the optional “Remove ads” purchase.
  • Advertising ID and Android ad services: used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads.

It does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

6. How we use and share information

We do not sell your personal information. We don’t run our own servers that collect data about you. The information described in section 3 is processed by Google as our service provider and under Google’s own policies. We may disclose information if required by law.

7. Data retention and security

Data stored on your device stays there until you clear it or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google services is kept according to Google’s retention policies. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to handle your request. Information sent by the app over the internet is encrypted in transit.

8. Children

The app is a service book for worshippers of all ages and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Clear or uninstall the app to remove all on-device data.
  • Reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in Android Settings.
  • Buy “Remove ads” to stop the app showing ads.
  • Ask us to access or delete any emails you sent us.

Depending on where you live, including under Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, you may have further rights over your personal data. Contact us to use them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top.

11. Contact
